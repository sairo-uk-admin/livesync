The game in one paragraph

Players buy, build and race their own motorsport team on Roblox. You start as a driver with one tired car and a shed. You race, earn, upgrade the car and grow the garage into a team with staff, a second driver and better facilities. On live weekends you race alongside real drivers, whose cars are streamed into the game as they race in real life.

Experience pillars (proposed)

The GDD leaves these blank. These four come from the GDD, the 7 October call and the reference games. They are listed as a decision so the team can agree or change them.

You are the driver
Every session includes driving. Upgrades are felt on track, not only read in a menu.
You can see your progress
Every upgrade is visible: new parts on the car, new equipment in the garage, a bigger building.
Racing the real thing
Live weekends put you on the same track as real drivers. Everything you build leads up to that moment.
Rivalry that slows you down but never robs you
Other players can hold you up but can never take away anything you have earned.

Progression tiers

Adapted from the brainstorm for a driver-first game. Shaded tiers are proposed for the pilot. How many tiers ship at Christmas is still an open decision.

Tier 1 · Pilot

Backyard racer

A shed, one second-hand car and basic tools. You drive every race yourself.

Unlocks: the first proper workshop and club races

Tier 2 · Pilot

Garage owner

Multiple bays, ramps and tools. Upgrade parts properly and start placing equipment.

Unlocks: weekend leagues and live ghost races

Tier 3 · Pilot / Phase 2

Club team

Hire your first mechanic, sign a sponsor and run a proper league season.

Unlocks: a second driver and the racing licence

Tier 4 · Phase 2

Pro team

Two cars and two drivers, a pit crew and R&D. Specialist rooms such as the dyno and paint booth.

Unlocks: regional live leagues

Tier 5 · Pipeline

Contender

Run at the front of live races. Driver academy, rival teams and big sponsors.

Unlocks: elite events and prestige cars

Tier 6 · Pipeline

Racing empire

Finish this vehicle class's regulation, keep your heritage cars and move to the next class.

The GDD's end state: "completed this regulation"

First session

Based on the call: race first, win no matter what, then drive to your plot. The tutorial should feel like playing the game.

0:00–0:30
A skippable story beat: you are thrown into a seat at the last minute. You are on the grid within seconds.
0:30–3:00
One-lap first race. You win however you drive, then a podium, trophy and first cash.
3:00–5:00
Drive to your plot: an empty shed with a ramp outline. Place your first equipment.
5:00–10:00
First upgrade (tyres). The new part is visible on the car. A time trial shows the gain against your own ghost.
10:00–15:00
First race against other players. The notice board shows the countdown to the weekend league.
15:00+
Offline earnings are introduced ("your garage keeps working while you're away"), plus a goal to unlock tier 2.
