1. Executive summary

Sports fans who game watch more sport. Football fans who play video games are 45% more likely to watch 3+ matches per week than casual fans, and spend an average of 10.3 hours gaming each week (GEEIQ, citing Sportcal 2024 and SQ Magazine 2025). Roblox could be a feeder channel to an always-on live hub.
The next generation of sports viewers is already on Roblox. 7-14 year olds spend more time on Roblox than on any other entertainment platform. The Sports & Racing category on Roblox hosts 1,857 games with 30.4B lifetime visits and 304k concurrent players - a global top-25 genre (Rotrends, Apr 2026).
The category is proven and the window is narrowing. 50 football brands have activated in virtual worlds, 87 branded football activations across platforms, integrations up 390% since 2024 (GEEIQ). FIFA acquired its own Roblox game. Adidas hosts 14 clubs in Football Festival. The Premier League launched a Roblox hub.
Branded attention on Roblox is 100x higher than social ads, with 211% better brand recall (MediaScience 2025). A 10-minute session is worth 20x a 30-second TV spot.
Four metrics are modelled below: impressions per session, session retention, player spend, long-term retention. Each is argued separately and combined in an interactive calculator (section 11).

6. The engagement time gap

A matchday pre-roll buys 15-30 seconds of passive attention. A Roblox hub buys 9-17 minutes of active engagement, repeated per session, across multiple sessions a week. The sports IPs in section 4 prove the session lengths are achievable.

Per-visit conversion

Session (min) x 60 / 30 = equivalent 30-second impression count.

At the NFL Universe Football benchmark: 16.85 min session = ~34 impression-equivalents per visit.

At the FIFA Super Soccer benchmark: 9.55 min = ~19 impression-equivalents per visit.

At the Rotrends top-10 sports average: 11.8 min = ~24 impression-equivalents per visit.

EMV methodology

Duration-adjusted impressions. No quality multiplier. The most conservative defensible approach - deliberately understated.

EMV = Visits x Impressions-per-visit x CPM / 1,000
  • Visits: monthly, from RoMonitor or creator dashboard once live
  • Impressions per visit: session-minutes x 60 / 30 (a 30-sec ad-equivalent unit)
  • CPM: £12.00 blended (Gupta Media 2025), conservative against linear sport

MediaScience supports a 2-3x premium - this quality multiplier is deliberately excluded.
