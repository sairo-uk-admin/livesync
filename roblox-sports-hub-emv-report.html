1. Executive summary

Sports fans who game watch more sport. Gamer fans are 45% more likely to watch 3+ matches per week than casual fans (GEEIQ / Sportcal 2024). Roblox is a natural feeder channel to live sport viewership.
Gen Alpha is already on Roblox. 7-14 year olds spend more time on Roblox than on any other entertainment platform, and the Sports & Racing category is a global top-25 genre with 30.4B lifetime visits.
The category is established. 87 branded football activations have launched across virtual platforms, up 390% since 2024. FIFA, Adidas and the Premier League have all made strategic moves into Roblox.
Attention quality is significantly higher than other digital channels. Branded experiences on Roblox generate 100x more attention than social ads and 211% better brand recall (MediaScience 2025).
This report models four metrics: impressions per session, session retention, player spend, and long-term audience retention. Each is evidenced separately and combined in the interactive calculator above.

6. The engagement time gap

A matchday pre-roll delivers 15-30 seconds of passive exposure. Comparable sports experiences on Roblox generate 9-17 minutes of active, opted-in engagement per session. The session length benchmarks in section 4 establish that these durations are consistent across the category.

Per-visit conversion

At the Rotrends top-10 sports average: 11.8 min = ~24 impression-equivalents per visit.

EMV methodology

The methodology converts session duration to impression-equivalent units, without applying a quality multiplier. This produces a conservative estimate relative to attention-adjusted approaches.

How EMV is calculated

The formula converts player session time into an equivalent number of 30-second ad impressions, then values those impressions using a standard media CPM rate.

EMV  =  Visits  ×  Impressions per visit  ×  CPM  ÷  1,000
Variable 1
Visits

Monthly unique visits to the hub, drawn from platform analytics (RoMonitor or creator dashboard).

Benchmark: FIFA Super Soccer 20.8M/mo · NFL Universe 8.6M/mo
Variable 2
Impressions per visit

Session length in minutes, converted to 30-second impression equivalents.

mins × 60 ÷ 30 = impressions
Benchmark: 9 min = 18 imp · 17 min = 34 imp
Variable 3
CPM (£)

Cost per 1,000 impressions. CPM is the standard unit used to price media inventory across TV, digital and in-game channels.

Default: £12 (Gupta Media 2025 blended Roblox rate) · Linear TV sport: ~£28

Note: no quality multiplier is applied. MediaScience data supports a 2-3x attention premium for Roblox vs social ads; excluding it keeps these figures conservative.

EMV = Visits × Impressions-per-visit × CPM ÷ 1,000
  • Visits: monthly, from RoMonitor or creator dashboard once live
  • Impressions per visit: session-minutes x 60 / 30 (a 30-sec ad-equivalent unit)
  • CPM: £12.00 blended (Gupta Media 2025), conservative against linear sport

MediaScience supports a 2-3x premium - this quality multiplier is deliberately excluded.

This pillar examines the long-term audience formation potential of an always-on live hub. The central question is whether sustained Roblox engagement translates into durable viewership habits. The metric is long-term retention; the conversion mechanism is the in-game-to-broadcast bridge.
